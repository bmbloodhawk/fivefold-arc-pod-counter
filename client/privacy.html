Privacy and your data

Guest play is available without an account. This page explains what this test build handles when you use it.

What you can use without an account

You can play as a guest. A pod code, active-table state, player-entered display names, and table choices are used to run that shared table. Your seat recovery credential is stored on your device so that device can reclaim its seat; do not share it.

Active test tables are not a backup service and may be lost if the service restarts. Do not use the app to store information you cannot afford to lose.

If you sign in

Sign-in is provided through Firebase Authentication. Fivefold Arc uses an opaque account identifier for its saved-data service rather than retaining the sign-in provider’s subject identifier there. Firebase may process the sign-in information needed to authenticate you under its own terms and privacy practices.

Your saved account data can include your preferred name, saved decks and private deck notes, personal appearance choices, saved game results, achievements, and any account preferences you choose to save. A saved game can include the table size, selected deck and commander names, recorded outcome, and a winner reason if one was entered.

Limited diagnostics and feedback

The test build may record limited technical diagnostics for a table, such as the app build, coarse viewport class, coarse device-memory class, visibility changes, and connection or rendering failure categories. It is designed not to record your IP address, user agent, screen capture, raw taps, card image, password, or full error message. Optional feedback is stored with the table context needed to investigate the report.

Card and rules lookups

When you ask the app to look up a commander, card, combo, or scanned card, the text or compact image you submit is sent only to the lookup service needed for that request. Current sources can include Scryfall, Commander Spellbook, and the card-image scanning service. Do not submit personal information in those fields.

Deletion and export

From My profile, signed-in players can export their saved account data or choose Delete my account data. Deletion permanently removes saved games, decks, preferences, and the server-side link to the signed-in account. It cannot restore deleted information or erase records that a third-party sign-in provider keeps for authentication.

Children and changes

Fivefold Arc is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or submit personal information for a child under 13 through this test build. If data practices materially change, this page will be updated before the new collection is enabled.
