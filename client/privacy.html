Privacy and your data

Guest play is available without an account.

Saved games and decks

If you choose to sign in and save a game, we store only your game result, table size, selected commander and deck, and any winner reason recorded for that result. Automatic finishing place is saved only when the counter can determine it from eliminations.

You can export or permanently delete your saved account data from My games. Deletion removes the saved games, decks, and the server-side link to your sign-in identity.

Table data

Your seat recovery credential stays on your phone. Active connected tables may be lost if the test service restarts.

Product measurement

Product measurement is not collecting data in this test build. If it is introduced later, this page will state the limited event categories, retention period, and opt-out before collection begins.
