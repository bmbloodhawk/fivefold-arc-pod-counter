What Fivefold Arc is

Fivefold Arc is a tabletop game companion and counter. It helps a pod record information; it does not make official game rulings, verify tournament results, or replace player agreement. Players are responsible for checking totals, resolving disputes, and following the rules that apply to their game.

Test-build reliability

This is an evolving test build. Features may change, be unavailable, or lose active-table state if the service restarts. Use it at your own discretion and keep another record for anything important. The app is provided as available, without a promise that it will be uninterrupted or error-free.

Your table and account

Use your own device and account credentials. Do not try to access, alter, or disrupt another player’s seat, account, or table. Do not share a pod code or QR invite beyond the people you intend to invite. A saved-seat credential is device-specific; keep it private.

Content and conduct

Do not use the service to submit unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or abusive content, or to interfere with the service. Keep private deck notes and player-entered names appropriate for the people at your table.
