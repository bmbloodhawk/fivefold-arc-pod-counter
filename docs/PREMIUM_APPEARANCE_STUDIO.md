# Create a premium skin

Open the desktop Studio with `qa/start-premium-studio.cmd` on Windows. It saves your drafts and release rehearsal history under **Fivefold Arc / Appearance Studio** in your local application data folder.

1. Start an editable **Neutral Arc** draft and name it. Import your source illustrations in **Asset library** and approve the images you intend to use. **Crop a copy** makes a tab icon, counter face, seal or thumbnail from the original.
2. In **Colors & mood → Commander identity**, select **Artwork regions**. Choose the surface, outline one part of the artwork, and finish the region. Choose which commander supplies the colors. Assign more regions to different color slots and try a partner pair, black, and colorless in the preview.
3. Fit the dial socket under **Game layout**. In **Counter faces & icons**, pick a counter and its face artwork. To keep the center upright while a different image turns around it, choose **Outer ring artwork**. Select **No separate ring** to return to the face's rotation settings. Under **Built-in tab artwork**, choose **Match skin** to follow the canvas pattern, or pick a set for all six tabs. Each tab also accepts its own image or glyph. Adjust player card backings under **Controls & cards** and check names and numerals in the live preview.
4. In **Review & publish**, choose your required achievements and save the draft. The game checks the account’s recorded achievements. Earned owners keep their skin across rule revisions.
5. **Prepare saved skin** captures its appearance, approved images and reward rule as one version. **Run release checks** exercises phone-sized game screens and isolated account scenarios. **Open practice preview** makes a playable table. **Phone QR** on the desktop Studio shares only that table on your trusted network until it expires or you choose **End playtest**.
6. Use **Download version** to carry the same file to the protected Studio on staging. Open the file, run its checks and stage it. After you approve the staging result, repeat on public. Public verifies the active version on staging before promotion. The local Studio labels both destinations as rehearsals.

To take a skin out of circulation, withdraw its active version. The Studio retains its history and ownership records. To restore an earlier version, open it from **Release history**, run its checks, stage it and promote it. The release panel displays the version fingerprint so you can compare environments. Public and staging require the Studio release feature to be deployed to those services once.
